Child Safety Standards

Align — Astrology & Insight  |  Last updated: April 2026

Our Commitment

Align — Astrology & Insight (“Align”) maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). We are committed to providing a safe environment for all users and take proactive measures to prevent, detect, and respond to any content or behavior that endangers minors.

Prevention Measures

Align implements the following safeguards to protect minors:

• All user-generated content, including posts, messages, and comments, is subject to content moderation and automated filtering to detect and remove prohibited material.

• Users must be at least 13 years of age to create an account. We reserve the right to terminate accounts suspected of being operated by underage users.

• Our community guidelines explicitly prohibit any content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors in any way.

In-App Reporting

Align provides in-app reporting tools that allow any user to flag content or accounts that may involve child safety concerns. Reports related to child safety are treated with the highest priority and are reviewed promptly by our team.

Enforcement & Account Actions

When CSAE content or behavior is identified or reported:

• The offending content is immediately removed.

• The associated account is permanently suspended without notice.

• Relevant evidence is preserved for law enforcement purposes.

Reporting to Authorities

Align complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations. When we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material (CSAM), we report it to:

• The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline (United States)

• Relevant regional and national authorities as required by local law

We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies investigating CSAE.

Staff & Training

Team members involved in content moderation and trust & safety are trained to identify and appropriately handle CSAE content in accordance with applicable legal requirements and industry best practices.
